# THE WORKS — Design Direction

## Dominant genre: Atmosphere
THE WORKS is a service-discovery storefront for three specialist providers. Use purposeful photography, confident editorial typography and a composed warm visual environment to make a diverse service range feel curated and approachable. Atmosphere is dominant on the public landing because the first task is to understand the offer and choose a relevant path.

## Secondary expressions by journey
- Quiet: search, request forms, customer account, quotations, confirmations and help.
- Data: company-specific owner/staff operations, records, payments, project tracking and reporting.
- Character: subtle motion, micro-feedback and campaign moments only; never let novelty interrupt a task.

## Visual language
- Palette: warm paper #F6F4EE, charcoal #20241F, deep green #294735 and amber #E5A044.
- Typography: Manrope for high-impact headings; DM Sans for body, navigation and controls.
- Photography: real, relevant imagery showing branded products, hospitality and event production. Use restrained image compositions and meaningful captions. Replace stock references with approved business-owned photography when supplied.
- Composition: compact sticky header; balanced split hero; service search and four shortcuts immediately after hero; searchable results identify the responsible provider; three distinct company cards; three-step explanation; shared-account reassurance; compact dark footer with one Briefcase icon.
- Interaction: search filters the curated service map, category shortcuts prefill search, result cards identify the provider and jump to that business section. Use native anchors and clear focus states.
- Motion: short hover/press feedback only; respect reduced-motion preference.
- Responsive: mobile-first single-column hero and company cards; two-column shortcuts; touch-friendly actions; no clipped descriptions.

## Content truth
- Do not publish unverified prices, service packages, ratings, availability, addresses or contact details.
- Clearly distinguish the three providers. Never imply common ownership.
- Use “Price on request” for future quote-led catalogues where prices are not confirmed.
- Do not present the inherited browser-local demo as a shared cloud account or live customer database.

## Reference adaptation
- Luvie's Foods: warm and direct brand voice, immediate customer action, image-led hero, compact menu-style discovery, explicit empty/loading states.
- The Plug: editorial heading scale, sticky navigation, curated catalogue/search layout, clear My Account route and discreet icon-only Briefcase affordance.
- Both are visual/interaction references only. Do not copy their brands, proprietary assets, route structure, authentication, database or business workflows.
